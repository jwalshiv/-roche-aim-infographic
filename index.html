The four layers

How the framework is structured

The prototype AIM Framework operates in four distinct layers (though more may be defined over time). Each layer narrows the content focus — from enduring strategic themes down to specific product portfolios.

Layer 01
Anchor
Long-term, Roche-centric core themes driven by strategic priorities. They don’t require regular updating, but serve as key ideas for more specific content to anchor to.
Innovation Collaboration Quality Digital +3 more
Layer 02
Buying Influence
How we identify and gather targeted content. Each buying influence has a distinct decision-making lens that shapes which anchors are most relevant.
Clinical Financial Operational
Layer 03
Disease Area Bridge
Content that connects anchors to disease areas. This layer translates strategic themes into clinically relevant narratives for specific specialties.
Neurology Oncology Infectious Disease +1 more
Layer 04
Portfolio Area Bridge
Content that connects anchors to portfolios. The final layer grounds strategic messaging in Roche’s specific product and solution areas.
Core Lab Molecular Lab Path Lab Point of Care Digital Solutions

The deliverable

Narrative output

The agent threads approved messages into a coherent narrative. AI is instructed to limit content generation as much as possible and to use approved content as much as possible. All content is cited as AI or Approved Content CM-XX. AI content is submitted to a candidate pool, where it can be approved or rejected for official use.
